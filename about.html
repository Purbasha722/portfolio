About

A little more context

The parts of the story that don't fit on a homepage.

I'm a Software Engineering student at Daffodil International University in Dhaka, currently maintaining a 4.00 CGPA. I got into this field the way a lot of people do — by wanting to build things — but it's the security side of software that keeps me up at night in the good way: thinking through what could go wrong, and designing so it doesn't.

Day to day, that means I care as much about how software is built as what it does — clean structure, sensible defaults, and a habit of asking "what happens if this input is hostile?" before I ship anything. I'm still early in the journey, so most of what I do right now is deliberate practice: small console tools, safety-focused apps, and a lot of reading about how systems fail.

Outside of code, I've spent years competing — in essay writing, in quiz competitions, even in shot put — and I bring that same "show up and prepare properly" mindset to engineering.
